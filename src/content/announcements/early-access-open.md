We're opening early access to ChaosEngine for a small group of design partners: pentest consultancies,
AppSec teams, and internal red teams who already run repeatable assessments and want them governed
rather than improvised.

## What early access includes

- Direct onboarding with the team building the product
- Help migrating one of your existing assessment workflows into ChaosEngine
- Documentation as it's written, ahead of public release
- A review of the registered tools and worker dependencies required by that workflow

## What we're asking for

Honest feedback on what breaks, what is missing, and which controls matter before the workflow can become
part of a real assessment process.

[Request early access](/early-access/) and tell us which workflow you would bring over first. Public
pricing is not yet published; pilot scope and commercial terms are confirmed directly before onboarding.
